Live Fatigue / Auto-Regulation — round-1 exploration

Four directions for the live in-set / between-set autoregulation signals. Rendered from synthetic objects shaped to the real WA outputs computeSessionFatigue, computeVBTSetFatigueIndex, and computeReadinessAdjustments. Fatigue is a 0–1 scale (teal = fresh → red = junk), kept visually distinct from the green→red VBT velocity scale. Designed for the SSE stream; degrades to a 500ms poll (see V4).

Fresh (level < .30) Building (< .55) High (< .75) Junk (isJunkVolume) · 4 scenarios: fresh · building · junk-crossed · load-drop
V1Radial fatigue gauge — ambient anchor

ReadinessCheck-lineage ring. Big centered score + zone word, band ticks on the arc, a rate-of-change caret, and a rep-tick sweep. Judge: reads gorgeously at a glance, but is weaker at showing where the junk threshold is and how fast you're approaching it than a linear meter — and it may be redundant beside the velocity strip.

V2Horizontal zoned meter — proximity to the junk line

TempoBar/IntensityBar-lineage segmented track with named zones and the junk line drawn explicitly; a needle slides to the current level, per-rep velocity ticks sit underneath. Judge: makes proximity-to-junk the primary read, but is denser and competes visually with the velocity strip's own bars.

V3Alert-forward card — quiet until threshold

Stays calm ("Productive · keep going") through fresh/building, then floods and pulses at the junk threshold with an assertive verb. Shown across all four states so the quiet→loud arc is visible. Judge: best signal-to-noise, but hides the continuous "how's it trending" read most of the time.

V4Auto-regulation coach panel — live vs. 500ms-poll

One between-set panel fusing gauge + junk/readiness chip + the load prescription (weightAdjustmentLbs → "40 → 30 lb"). Each decisive moment is rendered twice: a streaming treatment (continuously easing fill, pulsing live dot) and a 500ms-poll treatment (stepped fill, tick flash) — the direct comparison the SSE decision needs.

The two columns animate live — watch the fill: streaming glides & micro-updates every frame; poll snaps once per 500ms with an info-blue tick. Same data, same endpoint contract, different feedback cadence.
