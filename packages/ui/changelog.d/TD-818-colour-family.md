The colour family cells of decision 0004: `tint-{m}-solid` under `on-tint-{m}` and
`tint-{m}-subtle` under `on-tint-{m}-subtle` for red, orange, amber, green, cyan, blue, magenta and
neutral, in both modes, every value a ramp step. `brand-primary`, `brand-secondary` and
`status-success` / `-warning` / `-error` / `-info` alias their hue's cells for `-solid`, `on-*`,
`-subtle` and `on-*-subtle`. `WorkoutPill` paints `deload` on the magenta subtle cell. The
`Foundations/Color/Family` story shows the eight members in both modes with the ratios (TD-818).
