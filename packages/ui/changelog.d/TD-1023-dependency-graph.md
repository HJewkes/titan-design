`DependencyGraph`, from the new `@titan-design/react-ui/dependency-graph` subpath (not the root): a system's units boxed by tier with routed dependency edges, on a layout computed before render, so no layout runs in the browser and it server-renders and hydrates. `graph` takes the nodes, edges, tiers and layout a build step produces. Pressing a unit, or Enter on it, focuses it: its direct dependencies, its dependents and the edges to them brighten to `text-primary` while every other mark stays in `border-input`, so every mark clears 3:1, and a panel names them beside the consumer's `renderNodeLink` slot. `egoId` draws one unit's neighbourhood, for a page about it. Focus is `focusedId`, `defaultFocusedId`, `onFocusedIdChange`. Pure helpers ship with it: `egoSubgraph`, `neighbourhood`, `cleanDependencies`, `dependencyEdgeId`, and `fromCompoundLayout`, which adapts a compound layout result (the shape `computeCompoundLayout` returns) to `graph.layout`. `NetworkGraph` gains two optional colours: `GraphEdgeKind.color` paints a kind's edges and their arrowhead at rest, and `GraphGroupRegion.stroke` outlines a box group. Unset, it paints as before (TD-1023).
