`BeatStage` is a video beat's frame: an `AmbientStage` (the `.atmosphere-warm` brand washes and the `.grain` tile, both static) inside a `base` `Surface`, with `header`, stage and `chips` slots placed by `stageBox`. The stage and chip band are fit-checked in development. `units: 'canvas'` (the default) lays slots out on a 540-high design canvas scaled to the frame. `atmosphereWashes` and `stageGrain` are the pure material parts (TD-885).
